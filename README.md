# UrPath 🦈

> Helping students discover opportunities, understand their direction, and make the most of their university years.

**UrPath** is a student-led initiative built to help university students discover
extracurricular opportunities, explore their academic and career paths, and make
more intentional decisions throughout their university journey.

What started with four people and a simple idea has grown into a student community
that has reached more than **8,000 students**.

---

## Our Story

UrPath started in **October 2024** during a training program organized by **Azm
in Saudi Arabia**.

We started as a team of four people after noticing a recurring problem:

Many students wanted to develop themselves, gain experience, and participate in
opportunities outside the classroom, but they often did not know where to start.

Opportunities existed.

Students simply did not always know where to find them, which ones were worth
pursuing, or how those experiences could contribute to their future.

So we built **UrPath**.

At first, our mission was simple:

> Make valuable opportunities easier for students to discover.

But as our community grew and we started listening more closely to students,
we realized that finding an opportunity was only part of the problem.

Many students were also asking a much bigger question:

> **Where am I actually going?**

That question gradually shaped UrPath into something bigger than an
opportunity-sharing initiative.

---

## What UrPath Does

UrPath connects students with extracurricular opportunities and experiences such as:

- Competitions
- Hackathons
- Training programs
- Volunteering opportunities
- Student programs
- Events
- Communities
- Workshops
- Academic and career resources
- Skill-building opportunities

But sharing opportunities is only one part of what we do.

UrPath also focuses on helping students understand **how to use those opportunities
intentionally**.

We talk about university life, personal development, career preparation,
exploration, decision-making, and the experiences students can build before
graduation.

At the center of UrPath is one question:

> **How can students make better use of their university years?**

# UrMajor 🎓

## Helping Students Understand Their Academic Path

As we interacted with more students through UrPath, another recurring challenge
became impossible to ignore:

Many students were struggling to choose their academic major or understand which
path actually suited them.

Choosing a major can be a difficult decision, especially when students are still
discovering themselves.

Their interests may be changing.

They may be interested in several fields at the same time.

They may be influenced by trends, people around them, expectations, or the huge
amount of information they see online.

Sometimes a temporary curiosity can feel like a long-term passion.

Other times, a student may genuinely have several interests and have no idea how
to choose between them.

And simply telling someone:

> *“Follow your passion.”*

does not necessarily solve the problem.

That is why we created **UrMajor**.

UrMajor was a focused initiative designed to help students explore academic majors
and career paths with more clarity.

---

## Our Approach

The purpose of UrMajor was never to choose a major **for** a student.

Instead, we wanted to help students understand two things better:

**themselves and their options.**

We encouraged students to think about questions such as:

- What am I genuinely interested in?
- What skills do I already have?
- What skills am I willing to develop?
- What kind of problems do I enjoy solving?
- What type of work environment suits me?
- What does this major actually involve?
- What will I study?
- What career paths can it lead to?
- What opportunities exist in this field?
- Does this path fit my abilities, circumstances, and goals?

We wanted students to move away from choosing a path based only on its name,
popularity, or what other people expected from them.

Instead, we encouraged **informed exploration**.

---

## What We Created Through UrMajor

We developed detailed resources dedicated to explaining different academic
majors and career paths.

These resources included information about:

- What each major is really about
- What students study in the major
- Important skills for the field
- Possible career paths
- Future opportunities
- Areas of specialization
- Learning resources
- Courses and platforms students can explore
- Ways to experience the field before committing to it

We did not want students to read a two-line description of a major and make one
of the biggest decisions of their academic life.

We wanted them to **explore it properly**.

---

## More Than Guides

UrMajor was not limited to written resources.

We also:

- 📚 Created detailed guides for academic majors
- ✍️ Published educational articles
- 🎤 Organized workshops
- 🧭 Produced academic and career guidance content
- 🔎 Shared learning resources for different fields
- 💬 Discussed students' questions and concerns
- 👂 Listened directly to the challenges students were experiencing

Listening was one of the most important parts of the initiative.

Instead of assuming that every student had the same problem, we tried to
understand their individual situations.

Because two students considering the same major may have completely different
strengths, interests, circumstances, and goals.

---

## The Idea Behind UrMajor

UrMajor was built around a simple belief:

> **Students do not need someone to decide their future for them.  
> They need better information, better questions, and enough space to understand
> themselves before making the decision.**

The goal was not to give students a perfect answer.

It was to give them **clarity**.

UrPath helps students discover opportunities.

**UrMajor helped them think about the direction in which those opportunities
could take them.**

---

## Building a Student Community

One of the most important parts of UrPath has always been its community.

We never wanted students to simply receive an opportunity link and leave.

We wanted to create an environment where students could:

- Discover new possibilities
- Ask questions
- Exchange experiences
- Learn from each other
- Find useful resources
- Explore different paths
- Feel encouraged to try something new
- Think more intentionally about their future

Over time, UrPath became a growing student ecosystem rather than simply an
opportunity-sharing page.

Today, more than **8,000 students** have been reached through UrPath and its
content.

## 🦈 The UrPath Shark

UrPath also has its own character:

**the UrPath Shark. 🦈**

The shark became one of the voices behind UrPath's student guidance and awareness
content.

Through the character, we talk to students about topics such as:

- University life
- Exploring opportunities
- Building experience early
- Developing useful skills
- Academic decisions
- Career preparation
- Common mistakes during university
- Getting outside the comfort zone
- Making better use of university years

The idea was to make guidance feel less like a lecture and more like advice from
someone who understands student life.

Simple.

Relatable.

Sometimes playful.

But always useful.

And yes —

**I am the shark. 🦈**
