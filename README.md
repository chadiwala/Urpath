
# UrPath 🦈

> Helping students discover opportunities, explore their paths, and make the most of their university years.

UrPath is a student-led initiative that connects university students with
extracurricular opportunities, guidance, communities, and experiences that
can help them grow beyond the classroom.

## Our Story

UrPath started in October 2024 during a training program by Azm in Saudi Arabia.

We began as a team of four people with a simple observation:

Many university students want to grow, gain experience, and explore opportunities,
but they often do not know where to start or where to find the right opportunities.

So we built UrPath.

What began as a small student initiative gradually grew into a community that has
reached more than **8,000 students**.

Over the past two years, we have helped students discover opportunities,
understand possible career and academic paths, and become more intentional
about how they spend their university years.

---

## What UrPath Does

UrPath focuses on connecting students with opportunities such as:

- Competitions
- Hackathons
- Training programs
- Volunteering opportunities
- Student programs
- Events
- Communities
- Career and academic guidance

But UrPath became more than an opportunity-sharing platform.

It became a student community built around one question:

> How can students make better use of their university years?

---

## UrMajor

While working with students, we noticed another recurring problem:

Many students were unsure about their majors, career paths, and the options
available to them.

To address this, we launched **UrMajor**, a limited initiative dedicated to
helping students better understand:

- University majors
- Career paths
- Different fields
- The relationship between majors and careers
- Which paths may fit their interests and strengths

UrMajor was created because we saw the problem directly inside our community.

Instead of assuming what students needed, we built something around a need
we repeatedly observed.

---

## Building a Student Community

One of UrPath's biggest achievements has been its community.

We did not want students to simply receive opportunities and leave.

We wanted to create a space where students could:

- Discover new possibilities
- Learn from each other
- Ask questions
- Share experiences
- Find guidance
- Feel encouraged to try new things

Today, thousands of students have interacted with UrPath and its content.

---

## 🦈 The UrPath Shark

UrPath also has its own character: **the UrPath Shark**.

The shark became the voice behind much of UrPath's student guidance and
awareness content.

Through the character, we share advice about:

- University life
- Building experience early
- Exploring opportunities
- Developing skills
- Avoiding common mistakes
- Career preparation
- Making better use of university years

The goal is to make guidance feel simple, relatable, and student-friendly
rather than formal or overwhelming.

And yes — I am the shark. 🦈
